Two kinds of breaking, named apart. Explicit, the tilted table: the RED knob gamma tilts the table towards x and the marble rolls there; the rule itself prefers x, no choice of ours involved; [H, Q] != 0. Spontaneous, the falling pencil: the rule is even-handed between +x and -x (the mirror F = prod_j sigma^z_j, a half turn of every needle about z, leaves the energy unchanged), but a balanced pencil does not stay up: the infinite chain falls one way, stripes starting +x or their mirror image, same energy; [H, F] = 0, F|GS> != |GS>. Two borrowed facts on the cards. Gap => exponential decay (Hastings-Koma 2006): a gapped ground state of a chain with neighbour couplings has connected correlations that fall off exponentially; the fading length is 1/(2M) on the repulsive side and set by the bound pair, 1/m_B1, on the attractive side (3.10b); the same ingredient gave the area law in 1.9. Baxter/JKM: M known exactly: Baxter (1972) and Johnson, Krinsky and McCoy (1973) solved this chain and M follows at any Delta_z, gamma of the strip |Delta_z| < 1, gamma > 0 (elliptic functions, Ledger; again in 4.8); on the XY line M = atanh gamma (the yellow line of the Map lights, its star at the foot, gamma = 0); every M in these talks is this exact value, never a fit. The knob is back home at gamma = 0.2.
An error in the stripes: a single bond where two neighbours point the same way (the orange marker, 'a misprint in the stripes', shown large in an inset); left of it the stripes, right of it their mirror image (the F of 3.3): a wall between the two falls of the pencil, a kink. It slides: one site over, then bond by bond, and the stripes on both sides stay perfect. Zoom: the wall is not sharp; over about 1/M sites the needles shrink and turn over (tanh profile, cartoon), size ~ 1/M; at home 1/M = 9.2 sites (M = 0.10836, Delta_z = 0.3, gamma = 0.2, paper: jkm_exact.py), the second ruler of 3.7; beside it 3.9 sites (M = atanh 0.25 = 0.2554, XY line, Delta_z = 0, gamma = 0.25, closed form) and 2 sites (M = 0.5, cartoon). The price: M is one over a length, an energy needs a speed; E_kink = v M, energy cost = v M, v = 4 on the XY line (closed form). The kink is the chain's atom of error and v M is its price.
Can you make just one kink? Perfect stripes, 64 needles (the middle magnified below); a sigma^z stamp turns the needle at site 32 half a turn about z, and the bad bonds are two, one on each side: a kink (orange) and an antikink (maroon), walls: 2. They fly apart, the stretch between them holds the mirror stripes, they meet and cancel, the stripes heal, walls: 0 (a cartoon, gen_kinks.py, wall width 1/M at Delta_z = 0.3, gamma = 0.2). The rule: anything local makes walls in pairs; far left and far right the stripes start with plus and nothing local changes what happens at infinity, so the number of walls in between is always even. At home, where walls repel, the lightest thing a local stamp can make is a pair, costing two v M (closed form: twice 3.8's price).
Two walls on one chain: do they feel each other? Only the white rod Delta_z decides today. The pair's energy against distance, in units of the single-wall cost vM: far apart it is 2 (two free walls, closed form). Delta_z > 0: the cartoon curve comes down to 2 from above, pushing the walls together costs energy, they repel (home, Delta_z = 0.3, gamma = 0.2). Delta_z < 0: the curve dips below 2 at a finite distance, the walls drift to it, they attract. Delta_z = 0: flat, they ignore each other: the free-fermion line of the Map. Shapes are cartoons; the sign is the physics; written on the knob: negative attracts, positive repels; home is on the repulsive side.

Part 2's question mark is filled. A window's charge, measured many times, is a bell; with a short memory its variance grows like a straight line in ell (each new site brings the same extra width); twice the slope is chi_zz (the yellow line of slope 0.0553 is the infinite chain at large ell, iDMRG; the coins' ell/4 is steeper). Second form: half the sum over every distance of the connected bars of 3.7 (the fan from one site, ell such fans, up to the window's edges). On the ten-spin ring the computer adds them: 1/2, then -0.379, +0.044, -0.026, ..., landing at 0.145 (ring toy, pinned, L = 10; Delta_z = 0.3, gamma = 0.2): a toy, a ring of ten cannot hold a 4.6-site memory. The infinite chain gives 0.1106 (iDMRG, next scene), drawn apart. Rule card: chi_zz is called a susceptibility; for a conserved charge the answer to a small field is the width per site divided by the temperature; here the charge is not conserved and chi_zz is strictly the width per site, at T = 0 (for this charge the field answer is the static Kubo moment, a different number).
The hand-off: the variance is a sum over excited states, one term per rung of the ladder, Var(Q_A) = sum over k != 0 of ||^2 (the letter k is not spoken). On the cartoon ladder the bound pair (pink, marked 'Delta_z < 0 only': it exists on the attractive side, not at the repulsive home point of the numbers on the right) and the kink pair (orange/maroon) are the lowest rungs and carry the longest weight bars; nothing is derived here, 4.6 opens the formula and shows why the lightest weigh most. The ring accumulator (0.145, ring toy) and the iDMRG value 0.1106 stay on the right, apart.
A wavefunction of L spins is a list of 2^L numbers: 1,024 at L = 10, 1,048,576 at L = 20, about 1.8 x 10^19 at L = 64, hopeless (closed form). The trick is a different shape: one small table per site joined by wires with D numbers running through each wire (the train). Two moves, repeated: grow (two fresh sites slot into the middle, the chain settles; infinite = the pattern repeats) and compress (at a wire, the eigenvalue bars of Part 1 for that cut fall steeply; a gapped chain has few that matter, so keep the biggest D and drop the rest; L d D^2 << 2^L: 64 x 2 x 128^2 is about 2.1 x 10^6 against 1.8 x 10^19). JPEG for wavefunctions. Is D big enough? Raise it and watch the digits: at the hardest point of the Map, Delta_z = 0.5, gamma = 0.1, chi_zz reads 0.033726, 0.033723, 0.033723 at D = 64, 96, 128 (paper: chi_conv.json; iDMRG, infinite chain). Locked. Black box: iDMRG (everything inside): the train is a matrix-product state run with TeNPy; keeping the largest D bars at every cut is why it works for gapped chains (area law, 1.9); convergence is judged by raising D until the digits stop moving, and chi_zz is read from correlator sums in the converged state.
Three things to take home. Gamma tilts the table (explicit) and manufactures the red corners, the breaking the meter cares about; the stripes are one fall of the pencil (spontaneous). A gap buys a short memory: correlations fade over 1/(2M) sites, errors are kinks of size 1/M costing vM, made in pairs; M is exact (Baxter) and an inverse length. chi_zz is the width of the charge bell per site, twice the slope of the variance against ell or half the sum of the z correlations; 0.1106 at home (iDMRG, infinite chain; Delta_z = 0.3, gamma = 0.2).
