Part 3's closing sum returns: the variance is a sum over excited states, one term per state; call chi_zz the budget (the yellow outline). The factory's double flip: gamma flips two neighbours at once and the charge jumps by two (counter on one z snapshot of Octo's stripes); the charge leaks, Q-dot = i[H, Q] != 0.
